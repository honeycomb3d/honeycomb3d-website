Our story

Started over a bad batch of nylon

Honeycomb 3D was founded by a mechanical engineer who specializes in automated machine design. The business began with an order of nylon parts from a national print service that came back below standard.

That order made the gap clear: businesses in our area needed engineering-grade prints done properly, by someone who understands what the part has to do, without national-service pricing.

So we built that service. We focus on functional materials, we review parts with an engineer's eye, and we keep costs low by staying local and lean.

What that means for your parts

  • Material advice from a working mechanical engineer
  • Engineering materials as the focus, not an add-on
  • A local shop you can reach directly
  • Pricing built for repeat business work
