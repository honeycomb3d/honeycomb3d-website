Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive what you type into it: your name, company, email address, phone number if you give one, and your project details. If you email us or send files, we receive those too.

We do not ask for payment details through this website, and the site has no user accounts.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare quotes, and make and deliver your parts. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list.

Files and project details you send us are kept confidential and used only for your quote or order.

Outside services this site uses

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form submissions to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it logs technical details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the heading font. Your browser requests it from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • The MakeGood community map on our Community page is provided by 3D-Mobility.org, which records visits to the embedded map.

The donation button on our Community page takes you to 3D-Mobility.org. Donations are handled there, under their own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers of its own. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your own browser and is never sent to us.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries and order correspondence for as long as we need them for business records.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email ian@honeycomb3d.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
