Community outreach

Putting our printers to work for good

The same machines that make parts for our customers also make things for people who need them.

Featured project

Make Good toddler mobility trainers

The toddler mobility trainer is a 3D printable design from Make Good that gives young children with mobility challenges a way to move on their own. We have printed and assembled two so far.

The toddler mobility trainer is an open design from MakeGood. The trainers we build are not certified medical devices.

Support the work

Help us print more trainers

Each trainer takes several spools of filament. Donations made through 3D-Mobility.org in our name become filament credit, so every dollar goes into material for the next build.

Donate filament

Where your donation goes

Funds raised through 3D-Mobility.org are redeemable only as filament credit at School House Spools. We donate the printer time, assembly and hardware.

Donations are processed by 3D-Mobility.org, not by Honeycomb 3D. We make no claim that they are tax-deductible.
